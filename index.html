PEEL Worksheet: Role of Government

Sec 3 G3 Social Studies

Build one strong paragraph at a time. Focus on one main factor, explain it clearly, use a specific Singapore example, then link it back to the question.

P = one clear main factor E = explain the idea clearly E = use a specific Singapore example L = show how it answers the question

1. Choose a question

Maintains law and order

Focus on how the government keeps people safe, ensures people obey laws, and creates a peaceful and orderly society.

2. Think before you write

1
Notice
What is the question asking the government to do?
2
Think
What is one strong Singapore example I can use?
3
Check
Am I explaining or only naming?
Tip: Start with “One way the government works for the good of society is by...”
Tip: Do not stop at naming the factor. Explain what the government does and why it matters.
Tip: Be specific. Name an agency, policy, programme, agreement, or real example in Singapore.
Tip: Use your main factor again and connect it to safety, well-being, or national security.

Success criteria

P Identify one clear main factor and answer the question directly.

Explain Describe the factor and explain the term or concept so your reader understands what it means.

Example Use a specific Singapore example to show the factor clearly.

Link Use the main factor again and show how it helps the government work for the good of society.
